Style B — what it gives you

Warm and human. Organic yellow blob grounds the doctor photo, purple highlights turn the name into a friendly accent, script-style handwritten touches add personality. Testimonial-and-relationship forward rather than corporate.

  • Primary: purple (#7C3AED) for CTAs and name highlight
  • Accent: warm yellow (#FACC15) behind the photo
  • Display / body font: DM Sans
  • Script accents: Caveat (handwritten)
  • Mood: friendly, modern, approachable — works well if you want patients to feel welcomed more than impressed
